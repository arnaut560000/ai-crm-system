## Product Name

AI CRM System

## Short Description

AI CRM System is a simple WordPress CRM plugin for tracking leads, follow-ups, notes, deal value, and pipeline status directly inside your WordPress dashboard.

## Who It Is For

AI CRM System is built for:

- Local businesses
- Freelancers
- Agencies
- Consultants
- Service providers
- Small teams that want a simple lead tracker inside WordPress

## Main Benefits

- Keep lead records inside WordPress admin.
- Track lead status, source, deal value, and follow-up dates.
- Add notes and activity history to lead records.
- Search, filter, update, and delete leads quickly.
- Import leads from CSV.
- Export leads to CSV.
- View dashboard analytics for lead mix and pipeline value.
- Configure currency, default status, records per page, and uninstall cleanup.

## Installation

1. Download the plugin ZIP.
2. Open your WordPress admin dashboard.
3. Go to `Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin`.
4. Upload the ZIP file.
5. Click `Install Now`.
6. Activate `AI CRM System`.
7. Open `AI CRM` from the WordPress admin menu.
8. Go to `AI CRM > Settings` to configure your defaults.

## Requirements

- WordPress 6.0 or newer
- PHP 7.4 or newer
- Administrator access
- MySQL or MariaDB supported by WordPress
